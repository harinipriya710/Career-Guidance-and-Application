Medical Healthcare & Medical Sciences

NEET UG

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test

Purpose: Single entrance for MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS across India.

Eligibility: 12th PCB, 50% marks (40% SC/ST/OBC).

Pattern: 180 questions, 720 marks, 3 hours, offline.

Website: neet.nta.nic.in

AIIMS

All India Institute of Medical Sciences

Purpose: Premier medical education through NEET counseling.

Eligibility: Top NEET ranks for AIIMS counseling.

Advantage: World-class education, research opportunities.

Website: aiims.edu

NEET PG

Postgraduate Medical Entrance

Purpose: Entry to MD/MS courses nationwide.

Eligibility: MBBS degree, completed internship.

Specializations: Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, etc.

Website: nbe.edu.in

Engineering Technology & Engineering

JEE Main

Joint Entrance Examination

Purpose: Gateway to NITs, IIITs, qualifier for JEE Advanced.

Eligibility: 12th PCM, 75% marks, max 3 attempts.

Pattern: CBT, 90 questions, 300 marks, twice yearly.

Website: jeemain.nta.nic.in

JEE Advanced

Indian Institutes of Technology

Purpose: Exclusive entrance for IITs, ISM Dhanbad.

Eligibility: Top 2,50,000 JEE Main qualifiers, max 2 attempts.

Advantage: World-class education, global recognition.

Website: jeeadv.ac.in

GATE

Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering

Purpose: M.Tech, PhD admissions and PSU recruitment.

Eligibility: B.Tech/B.E. completed or final year.

Opportunities: IITs, NITs, PSUs like ONGC, ISRO.

Website: gate.iitd.ac.in

Civil Services Government & Public Service

UPSC CSE

Civil Services Examination

Purpose: IAS, IPS, IFS and Group A central services.

Eligibility: Graduate, age 21-32 years.

Stages: Prelims, Mains, Interview, 6 attempts.

Website: upsc.gov.in

SSC CGL

Combined Graduate Level

Purpose: Group B & C central government posts.

Eligibility: Graduate, age 18-32 years.

Posts: Income Tax Officer, Auditor, Inspector.

Website: ssc.nic.in

Banking Exams

IBPS PO, SBI PO, RBI Grade B

Purpose: Officer and clerical positions in PSBs.

Eligibility: Graduate, age 20-30 years.

Exams: IBPS, SBI, RBI, NABARD recruitment.

Website: ibps.in, sbi.co.in

TNPSC

Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission

Purpose: State administrative services in Tamil Nadu.

Eligibility: Graduate, Tamil Nadu domicile required.

Posts: Group I, II services, VAO, AE positions.

Website: tnpsc.gov.in

Railway Exams

RRB NTPC, Group D, JE, ALP

Purpose: Technical and non-technical posts in Railways.

Eligibility: 10th/12th/Graduate based on post.

Posts: NTPC, JE, ALP, Group D positions.

Website: rrbcdg.gov.in

Defense Services

NDA, CDS, AFCAT

Purpose: Officer entry into Army, Navy, Air Force.

Eligibility: 12th pass (NDA), Graduate (CDS).

Training: NDA, IMA, Naval Academy, AFA.

Website: upsc.gov.in

Professional Law, Design & Management

CLAT

Common Law Admission Test

Purpose: Entry to National Law Universities for LLB.

Eligibility: 12th any stream, 45% marks.

Subjects: English, Legal & Logical Reasoning.

Website: clat.ac.in

NATA

National Aptitude Test in Architecture

Purpose: Gateway to B.Arch programs nationwide.

Eligibility: 12th PCM, 50% marks, drawing aptitude.

Skills: Mathematical aptitude, spatial visualization.

Website: nata.in

CAT

Common Admission Test

Purpose: MBA admissions to IIMs and top B-schools.

Eligibility: Graduate, 50% marks (45% SC/ST).

Career: MBA → Management Consultant → CEO/CXO.

Website: iimcat.ac.in

CUET UG

Central Universities Entrance Test

Purpose: UG admissions in central universities including DU.

Eligibility: 12th pass, subject-specific requirements.

Coverage: Arts, Commerce, Science streams.

Website: cuet.samarth.ac.in

NET/JRF

National Eligibility Test

Purpose: Assistant Professor eligibility and JRF.

Eligibility: Postgraduate, 55% marks (50% SC/ST).

Career: Teaching → Research → Professor → Academia.

Website: ugcnet.nta.nic.in

NIFT

National Institute of Fashion Technology

Purpose: Admission to fashion and design courses.

Eligibility: 12th any stream, age 23-30 years.

Courses: Fashion Design, Textile, Management.

Website: nift.ac.in

Eligibility: Graduate, Karnataka domicile required.

Posts: KAS, KPS, FDA, SDA positions.

Website: kpsc.kar.nic.in

BPSC

Purpose: Bihar state administrative services.

Eligibility: Graduate, Bihar domicile preferred.

Posts: BAS, BPS, Commercial Tax Officer.

Website: bpsc.bih.nic.in

Gujarat Public Service Commission

Purpose: Gujarat state administrative services.

Eligibility: Graduate, Gujarat domicile required.

Posts: GPSC Class 1, 2 services, Dy Collector.

Website: gpsc.gujarat.gov.in

WBPSC

West Bengal Public Service Commission

Purpose: West Bengal state services.

Eligibility: Graduate, WB domicile preferred.

Posts: WBCS, Food Inspector, Statistical Officer.

Website: wbpsc.gov.in
